GUTS Scheduler

GUTS Scheduler is a private, single-household tool. It lets the household's own assistant software read and add events on the household's own Google Calendar, so family appointments, school runs and activities stay in one place.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users, no advertising, and it does not sell or share any data.

What it accesses: Google Calendar events only (the calendar and calendar.events permissions). It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
